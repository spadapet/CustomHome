A separate Miele combi-steam oven plus a GE microwave-and-wall-oven combination, with the same 30-inch Fisher & Paykel refrigerator and freezer columns, Frigidaire Professional dishwasher, Speed Queen laundry pair, and hood allowance. If the $4,000 cooktop ceiling includes tax, this choice is $238.94 over budget; see the lower-cost alternative below.

Induction cooktop choice

The likely pick is the Fisher & Paykel CI365ZZDTB5: a 36-inch, five-zone induction cooktop with two linkable SmartZones, PowerBoost, and an automatic cut-off timer for each zone. Fisher & Paykel lists it at $3,999 before tax; Sargent Appliance in Michigan also lists $3,999. Its center zone is rated 2,300 W, or 3,000 W on boost. Confirm the size of your largest pan or griddle against the actual cooking area, plus the countertop cutout and electrical service, before ordering.

What the estimate does not cover

  • The matching cabinet panels for the refrigerator and freezer.
  • The decorative cabinetry around the hood insert.
  • Installation, delivery, wiring, ductwork, and any additional appliances.

Details to decide or confirm

  1. Confirm the Fisher & Paykel CI365ZZDTB5's fit, electrical service, and price with the kitchen designer and retailer before ordering.
  2. Compare the separate cooktop and oven package with The Range Option, including cabinet layouts and installation quotes.
  3. Confirm the refrigerator and freezer model numbers, panel choices, water and ice connections, hinge directions, and combined installation width with the kitchen designer.
  4. Confirm whether the LG combination meets the desired microwave, baking, steaming, and Air Fry needs in person.
  5. Get a hood and ventilation plan that fits the chosen cooktop and cabinetry.
  6. Confirm the washer and dryer type, laundry space, hookups, and any other appliances in the full budget.
