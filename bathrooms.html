Home vision / Bathrooms

Bathrooms

Needs

  • Water on/off valves accessible for all sinks and toilets
  • Timer switch for the exhaust fan in every bathroom
  • Built-in night lighting in every bathroom, including powder rooms and all upstairs bathrooms. Provide a dim, warm light usable without the main lights, preferably automatic dusk/motion operation. A switch-integrated night light, illuminated receptacle or low-level vanity/toe-kick light are options; final product, location and controls remain undecided. Coordinate with the electrician, required GFCI protection and wet-location restrictions.

Ground-floor powder room

Enter from the common hall through a shared guest-entry alcove, not through the kids' room or coat closet. The powder room is now x = 0-7.1 ft, z = 35.1-41 ft, nominally 7.1 × 5.9 ft. Its east-wall opening is 30 inches wide and 8 ft high, z = 38-40.5 ft, centered between the alcove's finished wall faces to allow casing at both jambs. A painted-white door hinges at the south jamb and remains shown open 90 degrees inward by default; click its modeled leaf to toggle closed or to the 180-degree conceptual endpoint. Actual stops, fixture clearance and occupied circulation are unverified.

Centered reference-style vanity: retain the 42 × 21-inch oak Shaker vanity at (4.75, 36.225), facing south. Center the sink, faucet and round brass mirror on the actual counter midpoint, with equal counter space and symmetrically placed sconces. A 22-inch center sink cupboard has two doors; matching 10-inch drawer banks flank it, three narrow toiletry drawers per side, with small satin-gold knobs. This supersedes the offset sink and one-sided drawer bank. Keep the existing oak finish, single half-frosted window and towel hook.

The drawer boxes clear the basin, cupboard, towel and toilet through the modeled 15-inch extension with the entry door parked fully open. Close the right drawers before moving the entry door: extended right drawers intersect its sweep. Keep all drawers closed for the normal entry route. Fit the trap, bowl, slides, usable drawer interiors and hardware with the cabinet supplier before fabrication.

A brass hook on the east wall beside the vanity carries a hand towel, replacing the folded counter towel. The hook mounts 48 inches above the floor, north of the entry casing. Keep the northwest toilet (1.65, 36.45), low stone backsplash, warm tile and independent low-level night-light concept. The nominal 8-foot entry door retains its south-jamb hinge and 90-degree inward default pose.

Taller single privacy window: use one centered west-wall Andersen 100 Series standard-size 2640 casement at (0, 38.05), now modeled in a 2.5 ft wide × 4 ft tall minimum rough opening. Its bottom is 4 ft above the floor and top 8 ft, matching the door heads. The lower half stays frosted to 6 ft; upper half clear, with white inside/black outside. The manufacturer's 2024-25 size chart (dealer-hosted copy) gives an approximately 2.4583 × 3.9583 ft frame; the model uses this separately from the wall cut, with frame sill/head approximately 4.0208/7.9792 ft. Confirm the half-frosted treatment, actual sightlines/night privacy, handing, safety glazing, installation allowances and weatherproofing; standard dimensions are verified, not the complete product or installation.

The modeled toilet has a separate 30-inch-wide × 30-inch-deep forward approach beside the vanity, with approximately 42.3 inches from the bowl front to the south wall. A sampled 28-inch-diameter route reaches that approach with the entry door fully open; its full 0-90-degree swing clears the fixtures. Check actual door/frame clear width and occupied use; this geometry study is not accessibility approval.

A separate reach-in guest coat closet opens into the same alcove. The office north wall stays at z = 41; stairs, exterior footprint and room boundaries are unchanged. The alcove has approximately 37.8 inches between finished wall faces. Verify actual door/frame clear width, fixture and door-sweep clearances, basin construction, plumbing, exhaust, structure, lighting and electrical zones before construction. See the guest-entry model view and current reference.

Guest-bedroom shower bathroom

All bathroom doors have 96-inch heads. The guest shower has no door: fixed splash-glass panels flank a 30-inch open walk-in entrance. The glass is modeled 96 inches tall above the curb; verify actual panel sizing, safety glazing, mounting and water containment with the supplier.

Retain the adjoining walk-in closet. The bathroom is now x = 24.5-31.2, z = 34.6-43: nominally 6.7 × 8.4 ft, approximately 80.4 × 100.8 inches between modeled wall faces before additional finishes. Moving the bedroom-side wall back one foot returns 6.7 nominal square feet to the bedroom; the hall, exterior, queen bed and nightstands stay fixed.

Extend the shower across the available bathroom width, from x = 24.675 to 31.025, nearly wall-to-wall between finished faces. Its clear interior is now 73.8 × 36 inches (about 6 ft 2 in × 3 ft), with a modeled 76.2 × 38.4-inch outer pan/curb footprint. Remove the sliding doors entirely: fixed splash-glass panels flank a 30-inch open walk-in entrance, x = 26.7-29.2 at z = 37.925, aligned with the pocket entry and fixture aisle. The sampled 28-inch-diameter route stays clear. The retained curb must be stepped over; open-entry water containment, splash control and threshold details remain unverified. This is not curbless or accessible-shower approval.

Keep one sink on the 54-inch oak vanity at (30.2, 40.75), facing west, with a single centered 30-inch mirror. The centered 21-inch modeled basin and one faucet leave approximately 16.5 inches of nominal counter each side. Add a pair of warm 2700 K opal vanity sconces and retain the independent low-level warm night-light concept. Put a towel hook on the east wall beside the shower and south of the vanity, 48 inches above the floor, with a modeled 16 × 28-inch hanging towel. The compact 18-inch cabinet depth, mirror/sconce fit and hook placement are concepts; verify actual products, wet-location/electrical requirements, GFCI protection and mounting before construction. The centered 30 × 30-inch vanity standing zone shares the shower route: someone using the sink must step aside for shower access. Not simultaneous-use or accessibility approval.

The toilet remains at (25.8, 40.15), facing east, with approximately 31.2 inches to the vanity front. Pocket entry moves to z = 43, x = 26.7-29.2, nominally 30 × 96 inches with a 28-inch finished-clear target. The short west pocket-wall return reaches x = 24.05. Its schematic cavity, framing/header, wall thickness, partition return, services, nonpenetrating fixings and privacy hardware require architectural review. No swing returns. Timber trim stops behind wet and fitted fixtures. No tub; not construction or occupied-use approval.

NKBA planning guidance recommends at least a 36 × 36-inch shower interior and 30-inch clear space in front of fixtures; the modeled 36-inch shower depth meets that cited baseline, not code or product approval. See also KOHLER shower sizing. Nominal product sizes are not finished-clear dimensions. Verify actual open-entry clearance, occupied use, accessibility, splash control, water containment, waterproofing, plumbing, exhaust, night lighting and garage fire separation. Master Bathroom

Wants

  1. Built in tub, not too tall. Shower can be next to the tub. Windows by tub.

Inspiration

Use light natural-stained white oak for all vanities and linen cabinets, with Shaker doors and drawer fronts, frameless full-overlay construction, and satin-gold handles to match the cabinetry throughout the home. Confirm the stain and moisture-resistant protective finish on physical samples.
