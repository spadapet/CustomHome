Home vision / Bathrooms

Bathrooms

Needs

  • Water on/off valves accessible for all sinks and toilets
  • Timer switch for the exhaust fan in every bathroom
  • Built-in night lighting in every bathroom, including powder rooms and all upstairs bathrooms. Provide a dim, warm light usable without the main lights, preferably automatic dusk/motion operation. A switch-integrated night light, illuminated receptacle or low-level vanity/toe-kick light are options; final product, location and controls remain undecided. Coordinate with the electrician, required GFCI protection and wet-location restrictions.

Ground-floor powder room

Enter from the common hall through a shared guest-entry alcove, not through the kids' room or coat closet. The powder room is now x = 0-7.1 ft, z = 35.1-41 ft, nominally 7.1 × 5.9 ft. Its east-wall opening is 30 inches wide and 7 ft high, z = 38-40.5 ft, centered between the alcove's finished wall faces to allow casing at both jambs. A painted-white door hinges at the south jamb and is shown open 90 degrees inward, away from hall circulation.

Keep the toilet at (1.6, 36.65). Move the existing 27.6-inch vanity to the south wall at (3.25, 39.95), facing into the room; extend the tile with the room. A separate reach-in guest coat closet opens into the same alcove. Reclaim the 1-foot strip previously added to the office: its north wall returns to z = 41, while the stair-side buffer and exterior footprint stay fixed. The alcove has approximately 37.8 inches between finished wall faces; this is not accessibility or occupied-use approval. Verify actual door/frame clear width, fixture and door-sweep clearances, plumbing, exhaust, structure, and electrical zones before construction. See the guest-entry model view and current reference.

Guest-bedroom shower bathroom

Retain the 6.2 × 7.4 ft bathroom at x = 25-31.2, z = 34.6-42. The internal partition and tall linen cabinet stay removed; the adjoining closet returns inside its original footprint without occupying the hall.

Keep the northwest 36 × 48-inch shower, northeast toilet (29.7, 35.9), and southwest 24-inch vanity (26.05, 40.5), facing east. The nominal 30 × 84-inch bedroom opening stays x = 28.15-30.65, but its door now opens inward, west-jamb hinged on the bathroom frame face, shown 90 degrees open. The full sweep stays south of the shower and east of the vanity; the open leaf ends about 2.6 inches before the shower footprint. Toilet access remains beside the open leaf, not through the vanity zone. The shower glass door remains inward. Verify actual clear door widths, hinges/stops, occupied approach/turning, basin products, waterproofing, plumbing, exhaust, night lighting and garage fire separation. No tub. Explore the guest suite.

Master Bathroom

Wants

  1. Built in tub, not too tall. Shower can be next to the tub. Windows by tub.

Inspiration

Use light natural-stained white oak for all vanities and linen cabinets, with Shaker doors and drawer fronts, frameless full-overlay construction, and satin-gold handles to match the cabinetry throughout the home. Confirm the stain and moisture-resistant protective finish on physical samples.
