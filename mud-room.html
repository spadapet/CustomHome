Mud Room

A uniform, full-height U-shaped builder-built enclosure with warm ivory casework, soft putty backs, and light stained white oak on the centered coat-closet front, bench and drop surface. Gold hardware and oatmeal fabric containers finish the neutral palette. The owner has rejected green, including the former coat-front accent. Only finishes change: enclosed corner returns, aligned fronts and a continuous top rail still join the closet, shelves and five cubbies. No washer/dryer or built-in accessory drawers; the white-oak kitchen stays unchanged.

The mixed painted-and-stained-wood example by Amy Peltier in Homes & Gardens' mudroom ideas informed the material contrast, not the layout. This is a local finish proposal, not a door or paint-product selection. Confirm a matching stainable white-oak/veneer standard bifold and surround with the builder; the nominal door and mechanism stay unchanged. Review real paint, oak and fabric samples together in daylight and warm evening light, and confirm durable, washable finishes.

Needs

  1. Five wood-backed cubbies, each with two gold hooks mounted on its side dividers and one full-height, floor-level shoe opening below the bench. No bottom shelf, lip or intermediate shelf.

  2. A proper enclosed 36-inch-wide, 24-inch-deep coat closet with a conventional side-to-side rod, lower shoe shelves and upper storage. A standard nominal 32-inch bifold door is shown closed; final builder door/frame/hardware selection remains open.

  3. Five personal open shelf positions: Child 1, Child 2, Child 3, Mom, and Dad, children lowest. Each shows three small removable containers for socks, gloves and hats, bought separately rather than built-in drawers. Container allowance: 8.5 × 11 × 5.5 inches each; verify products against finished shelf openings.

  4. Joined open shoe shelves target 22 extra pairs across the two returns and above the accessory containers, in addition to daily shoes beneath the bench and two shelves in the coat closet. High shelves are seasonal/adult storage, not children's daily retrieval.

  5. Space to charge vacuum cleaners.

  6. Durable, waterproof, and easy-to-clean flooring.

  7. Durable, washable wall finish behind the bench and hooks.

  8. Drop zone for keys, mail, bags, and sunglasses.

  9. Space for backpacks without blocking the walkway.

Wants

  1. Motion-sensor lighting.
