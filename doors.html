Doors

Use painted-white craftsman-style casing around interior doorways and open passages, matching the window trim: wide flat side boards, a deeper header, and a small projecting cap and bead. Trim both sides of interior passages and the interior face of exterior door assemblies. Keep floor-level openings clear, without a sill or apron across the walkway. The dining patio door has a White leaf, jambs, header, and shared window/door mullion, with glass, dark hardware, and a dark threshold. Retain its inward hinging and closed display. Rotate the dining set 90 degrees and shift it 6 inches west to address chair interference through the full swing. The window bay immediately beside it also has White frames and sashes inside and outside, matching the door. Other windows retain Black exteriors and White interiors. Other door leaves and hardware retain their existing finishes. The 3D profiles are provisional; coordinate actual occupied circulation, cabinet junctions, casing returns, and door operation before construction.

Front entry lock: Wyze selected; exact model and finish remain undecided. Use one smart lock on the active leaf, not a smart lock on both leaves. The model's interior housing and active-leaf side are schematic. Confirm the door stile/glazing, bore/backset, latch system, inactive-leaf hardware, and free interior egress before fabrication. See the selection and installation checks.

Guest entry doors

For the separate guest-entry alcove, provide a nominal 30 × 84-inch painted-white powder door at x = 7.1 ft, z = 38-40.5 ft, south-jamb hinged and shown 90 degrees open into the powder room. Center its opening between finished alcove walls to allow casing at both jambs. The reach-in guest coat closet has a separate 48 × 84-inch opening at z = 37.5 ft, x = 7.65-11.65 ft, with two closed white-oak Shaker leaves and satin-gold pulls. Neither door requires entering the other room. Verify actual frame clear widths, hardware, swings, accessibility and fixture clearance. See the guest-entry layout.

The sweep clears the shower and vanity in the model. Shower glass still opens inward. Confirm actual units, clear widths, handing, occupied access, privacy, glazing and water containment with supplier/architect. See suite layout.

The house-to-garage opening is now nominally 36 × 96 inches, shifted 6 inches away from the truck to x = 39.5-42.5 ft at z = 26.9. Both vehicle doors already remain 9 feet wide × 8 feet tall. The garage exterior pedestrian door is also modeled 8 feet tall. Confirm rated house/garage separation, door unit, swing, self-closing/latching requirements, landing and step details; the house-entry leaf is not a selected product.

Office double doors

Match the latest reference photo with white 10-lite French doors: two columns by five rows of clear glazing per leaf, white muntins and substantial perimeter framing, round gold knobs, and dark hinges. Remove the former oak lower panels and privacy glass. Retain the nominal 5 ft-wide, 8 ft-high pair with two nominal 30-inch leaves and white Craftsman casing on both faces.

Opening x = 12.2 ft, center z = 45.25 ft, span z = 42.75-47.75 ft; no transom. Keep both leaves fully open inward at 90 degrees into the office, perpendicular to the opening, with conceptual hinges. The reference-photo 10-lite style remains; this supersedes both the closed and partly open displays. These nominal dimensions are not approved unit, rough-opening, acoustic, or clear-passage specifications. Confirm safety glazing, true/simulated divided lites, handing, jambs, active/inactive hardware, and occupied swings beside the loveseat. See office requirements.
