Brick and stone exterior

White and stone exterior

Linen brick / low pale stone palette

The owner selected 2 Linen from the palette sheet, superseding the Oatmeal color study. The low-stone decision stays: a continuous pale warm limestone wainscot ends at y=2 ft (24 in), with a separate 1.5-in warm off-white coping ending at y=2.125 ft (25.5 in). Heights reference the house floor, not grade or the lower garage floor. Actual east-prep sill-trim underside is 26.37 in, leaving 0.87 in clear; west living trim starts at 28.32 in and front cut-stone sills at 32.28 in. Do not raise the band to front glass level and bury lower side trim. Light Linen beige queen brick fills the remaining wall height across every elevation and return, including the raised patio headwall and fitted gable closures, cut around every fixed opening. Quiet putty mortar, warm off-white trim/coping #f3eee4, muted warm-charcoal metal/roof #595953 and retained natural oak keep the house light: no yellow/orange, pink or dark-brown overall scheme. At scan luminance 128, brick diffuse #d8cdbb, stone #d5d0c5 and roof #595953 match the concept swatches; these are texture calibration targets, not flat product colors. Photographed variation, lighting and physical samples change perceived color. Finished wall faces stay fixed, including the six-foot ground-level east fence clearance. Preserve all 53 window units, no shutters, roof forms, seven-foot porch, door poses, interiors and saved layouts. Retain stone at porch foundations, 30-in post bases, landing nose and risers; pale walking surfaces remain separate paving concepts. Brick uses an 8 × 3.125-in nominal face module with reduced relief; stone scan tile is an assumed 8 ft. Neither scale establishes an orderable product.

Materials are inspiration, not selections: reuse only the existing locally bundled CC0 Poly Haven brick/stone scans, with independent diffuse and relief maps; no manufacturer photography is used as a texture. Older Winterhaven, limewash and Rockford references remain historical candidates, not an approved match to this new palette. Require actual stone/brick/cream-gray mortar/coping sample panels in sun and shade, separate exterior slip/frost-rated paving and professional veneer support, anchors, cavity/transition flashing, weeps and drips. Roof skins are not framing: engineer cross-gable valleys, the curved framed entry header, post/ledger/uplift connections, foundations and the assumed 18-in main-roof framing allowance. Resolve valley snow/ice/runoff concentration at the porch, gutter/outlet sizing and lawful discharge. No product, construction, structural or permitting approval; do not support roof loads on veneer. Firepit remains cold storage only.

Twelve new 2700K recessed upper-soffit fixtures follow actual roof undersides; upper front gutter leaders move to outer corner stand-offs, clear of lower roofs. See whole-house window constraints, lighting and drainage routes. Veneer support, weathering/drips, cavity flashing, roof framing, wet-location installation, hydraulic capacity and below-grade discharge are not construction-approved.
