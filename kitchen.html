Home vision / Kitchen

Kitchen

Bright and highly functional family kitchen with great storage, durable finishes, and strong lighting. Family loves to cook, and we typically have about 3 people in the kitchen at the same time, so it needs to feel comfortable for multiple people working together.

Needs

  1. Kitchen sink facing the windows.

  2. Prep kitchen (countertop and sink) + pantry combined.

  3. Plenty of outlets for small appliances.

  4. Canned lights with diffusing cover.

  5. Undercabinet lighting.

  6. Durable, easy-to-clean surfaces and finishes.

  7. Lots of deep drawers instead of lower cabinets with doors.

  8. Pull-out cabinet for trash + recycling immediately to the left of the sink.

  9. Dishwasher immediately to the right of the sink.

  10. Vertical tray storage for cutting boards and baking sheets.

  11. Soft-close doors and drawers.

  12. Cabinets to the ceiling.

  13. Engineered wood flooring to match the rest of the main level.

  14. 36-inch farmhouse-style sink with an exposed apron front and a deep basin to prevent splashing.

  15. Under-sink storage solution for sponges, cleaning supplies, etc.

  16. Island seats 5 comfortably; the current concept uses 3 stools on the hall-facing side and 1 at each end.

  17. Two pre-drilled accessory holes for built-in hand soap dispenser and built-in dish soap dispenser

  18. Deep pull-out drawer for plastic containers.

  19. Deep pull-out drawers for plates and bowls.

  20. 36-inch induction cooktop.

  21. Fisher & Paykel 30-inch refrigerator and 30-inch freezer columns. The 3D concept separates the stair-side RS3084FLJK1 freezer and mudroom-side RS3084SRHK1 refrigerator with a landing niche, keeping both on the bottom wall. Handles face the niche; the stainless-panel finish remains a concept. Confirm separate-column installation, handing, panels, ventilation, and full door clearances before ordering.

  22. Microwave and/or air fryer.

  23. Cold drinkable/filtered water dispenser since some column fridge may not have them.

  24. Will need a large electrical panel capacity to accommodate appliances running.

  25. Second sink somewhere (like prep kitchen)

  26. Water leak sensor.

Wants

  1. Windows in the prep kitchen

  2. Paper towel holder inside a cabinet

  3. Might have to prep range for gas in case of resale.

  4. Prefer warm wood style kitchen

  5. Drawer with power outlets for phones, tablets, laptops.

  6. Instant drinkable hot water dispenser.

  7. Oversized dishwasher.

  8. Storage on both sides of the island.

Confirmed layout

Keep all four recessed under-stair bookcase bays and their open backdrop. The refrigeration wall starts beside them with full-height oak tower framing, while the existing kitchen wall remains full height at the turning-landing end-wall junction. Stair enclosures and guards remain unchanged, subject to architectural review.

Center the two-pane sink window between the kitchen's side walls, with the farmhouse sink and faucet on the window's centerline. Matching 18-inch upper cabinets flank the window with equal 4-inch gaps and aligned tops, moved outward 1 inch each to clear the white craftsman-style casing. The 3D sink wall has equal countertop lengths on either side of the sink; trash remains immediately left and the dishwasher immediately right. Use matching white casing around both kitchen passages; scribe cabinet-adjacent trim as needed during detailed design.

The bottom wall now has two separate 30-inch refrigeration towers around a 31 1/2-inch quartz landing counter, with a nominal 30-inch three-drawer bank below. Four 3/4-inch oak side panels frame the towers, and a 2.85-inch right-wall clearance zone keeps the refrigerator away from the mudroom casing. The columns remain 84 inches high; aligned cream-front storage cabinets and an oak cornice extend the composition to 9 ft 9 in. This replaces the adjacent pair and isolated right drawer bank without moving the island or reducing the stair bookcases. See the appliance package for model references. All cabinetry and clearances remain provisional.

Move both kitchen openings 6 inches away from their adjacent cabinet runs so the full white side casing remains exposed, rather than hiding behind the cabinets. Use the standard casing on both faces; the earlier mudroom trim build-out is removed. Appliance door swings still require verification.

Limit the warm ivory quartz backsplash to the central landing niche rather than a large field above the appliances. Add a shallow oak display shelf and recessed upper cabinet between the full-depth tower uppers. Leave the stair-side bookcase extension open above. The 3D concept includes a straight-on Fridge wall view that temporarily hides the island, pendant lights, and foreground cooktop hood for an unobstructed view.

The lower mudroom doorway now sits 6 inches toward the cooktop from its former countertop-aligned position. Its 3-foot width and 8-foot height are retained. Shorten the provisional coat cupboard another 10.2 inches at the doorway end to 27 inches wide, keeping its laundry-side end fixed so both the casing and future laundry reservation remain clear.

The prep-kitchen entry is a 2 ft 6 in-wide, 8 ft-high opening in a wall, not a gap across the full depth of the prep kitchen. Its sink-side edge is now 6 inches beyond the kitchen countertop front to expose the casing. The separate lower kitchen-to-mudroom passage is also 8 ft high and retains its 3 ft width.

The kitchen should read as a balanced layout in plan: matching full-width top and bottom runs, with the long island centered between them and the right-wall cooktop aligned with its midpoint. The bottom run includes the fridge/freezer, with base cabinets filling the remaining width; it is not a mirrored sink wall. Keep the hall side open and retain all five island seats. The 3D concept centers the island at z = 17.45 ft, leaving approximately 45 inches from each end of the countertop to the opposing runs. The cooktop run is shortened to preserve the existing prep-kitchen opening.

October 17, 2026: retain both refrigeration columns on the bottom kitchen wall, facing into the kitchen, not on the shared kitchen/mud-room wall. The latest concept separates them with a central landing niche instead of placing them directly adjacent. Keep an open passage through the shared wall near its bottom end, below the cooktop run, clear of appliances and mud-room storage.
