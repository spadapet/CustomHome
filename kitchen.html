Home vision / Kitchen

Kitchen

Bright and highly functional family kitchen with great storage, durable finishes, and strong lighting. Family loves to cook, and we typically have about 3 people in the kitchen at the same time, so it needs to feel comfortable for multiple people working together.

Start with four warm, dimmable 4-inch regressed/baffled recessed lights, not a dense ceiling grid. Place one for the sink zone, two along the cooking-side work aisle and one at the refrigeration approach. Retain the two island pendants and add actual downward task light beneath them.
