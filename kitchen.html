Home vision / Kitchen

Kitchen

Bright and highly functional family kitchen with great storage, durable finishes, and strong lighting. Family loves to cook, and we typically have about 3 people in the kitchen at the same time, so it needs to feel comfortable for multiple people working together.

Needs

The wall beside the tall refrigerator/pantry run projects approximately 11.6 inches farther into the hall/kitchen so its face aligns with the adjacent Shaker cabinet fronts, excluding handles. Its centerline is z = 24.7367 ft. Keep the cabinets and kids-room doorway in place; extend the room's side returns and coordinate the floor boundaries and shared first-floor layout. Structural and circulation review remains required.

  1. Kitchen sink facing the windows.

  3. Plenty of outlets for small appliances.

  4. Canned lights with diffusing cover.

  5. Undercabinet lighting.

Lengthen the island to 8 feet and use 24-inch stool centers instead of 20 inches. This gives 6-inch gaps between nominal seats; verify the selected stools and occupied circulation.

  19. Deep pull-out drawer for plastic containers.

  20. Deep pull-out drawers for plates and bowls.

  21. 36-inch induction cooktop.

  22. Fisher & Paykel 30-inch refrigerator and 30-inch freezer columns separated by the oven/storage tower on the bottom wall: RS3084FLJK1 freezer toward the mudroom wall, RS3084SRHK1 fridge toward the stairs. Handles face the tower. The stainless-panel finish remains a concept. Confirm separate-column installation, handing, panels, ventilation, hinge-side filler, and full door/drawer clearances before ordering.

  23. Microwave and/or air fryer.

  24. Separate hot and cold drinking/filtered-water dispenser beside the main sink faucet, not cold-only. Coordinate the filter, hot-water tank, power, plumbing, and under-sink storage.

  26. Second sink somewhere (like prep kitchen)

  27. Water leak sensor.
