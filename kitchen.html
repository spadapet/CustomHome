Home vision / Kitchen

Kitchen

Bright and highly functional family kitchen with great storage, durable finishes, and strong lighting. Family loves to cook, and we typically have about 3 people in the kitchen at the same time, so it needs to feel comfortable for multiple people working together.

Needs

  1. Kitchen sink facing the windows.

  2. Prep kitchen (countertop and sink) + pantry combined.

  3. Plenty of outlets for small appliances.

  4. Canned lights with diffusing cover.

  5. Undercabinet lighting.

  6. Durable, easy-to-clean surfaces and finishes.

  7. Lots of deep drawers instead of lower cabinets with doors.

  8. Pull-out cabinet for trash + recycling immediately to the left of the sink.

  9. Dishwasher immediately to the right of the sink.

  10. Vertical tray storage for cutting boards and baking sheets.

  11. Soft-close doors and drawers.

  12. Cabinets to the ceiling.

  13. Engineered wood flooring to match the rest of the main level.

  14. Approximately 36-inch farmhouse/apron-front sink with unequal double basins: prefer 30/70 or similar, never 50/50. Use a smaller left bowl and larger right bowl in the concept, with a low divider.

  15. Under-sink storage solution for sponges, cleaning supplies, etc.

  16. Island seating: four dark-frame stools with brown seats and wood backs, all in one row on the hall-facing side. No end stools. Retain the 7-foot length with compact 20-inch centers; comfortable four-adult seating remains unverified.

  17. Built-in soap dispenser beside the main faucet. The preview shows one pump; retain the original provision for a second hand/dish-soap dispenser when coordinating the final countertop hole layout. Do not pre-drill before selecting the sink and fixtures.

  18. Deep pull-out drawer for plastic containers.

  19. Deep pull-out drawers for plates and bowls.

  20. 36-inch induction cooktop.

  21. Fisher & Paykel 30-inch refrigerator and 30-inch freezer columns, side by side on the bottom wall: RS3084FLJK1 freezer left and RS3084SRHK1 fridge right, with handles together. A full-height pantry sits beside the pair. The stainless-panel finish remains a concept. Confirm paired-column installation and joining kit, handing, panels, ventilation, and full door clearances before ordering.

  22. Microwave and/or air fryer.

  23. Separate hot and cold drinking/filtered-water dispenser beside the main sink faucet, not cold-only. Coordinate the filter, hot-water tank, power, plumbing, and under-sink storage.

  24. Will need a large electrical panel capacity to accommodate appliances running.

  25. Second sink somewhere (like prep kitchen)

  26. Water leak sensor.

Wants

  1. Windows in the prep kitchen

  2. Paper towel holder inside a cabinet

  3. Might have to prep range for gas in case of resale.

  4. Prefer warm wood style kitchen

  5. Drawer with power outlets for phones, tablets, laptops.

  6. Instant drinkable hot water dispenser.

  7. Oversized dishwasher.

  8. Storage on both sides of the island.
