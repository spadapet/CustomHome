Home vision / Kitchen

Kitchen

Bright and highly functional family kitchen with great storage, durable finishes, and strong lighting. Family loves to cook, and we typically have about 3 people in the kitchen at the same time, so it needs to feel comfortable for multiple people working together.

Needs

The gray kids-room wall beside the refrigerator retains centerline z = 24.7367 ft and original end x = 29. Do not move that return or add a floor pocket there. The kitchen wall shared with prep and mudroom is at x = 37.5; its cabinetry and adjoining floor boundaries must coordinate without widening the oven bay. The kids-room doorway remains fixed. Structural, product and circulation review is required.

  1. Kitchen sink facing the windows.

  2. Prep kitchen (countertop and sink) + pantry combined.

  3. Plenty of outlets for small appliances.

  4. Canned lights with diffusing cover.

  5. Undercabinet lighting.

  6. Durable, easy-to-clean surfaces and finishes.

  7. Lots of deep drawers instead of lower cabinets with doors.

  8. Pull-out cabinet for trash + recycling immediately to the left of the sink.

  9. Dishwasher immediately to the right of the sink.

  10. Compact dish rack on the counter right of the main sink, above the dishwasher: stainless-look wire frame and a neutral removable 16 × 14-inch catch pan with raised edges. No drain spout; remove the pan and empty water manually. Keep the faucet and soap dispenser clear; confirm actual dimensions, pan removal, and cleaning access before purchase.

  11. Vertical tray storage for cutting boards and baking sheets.

  12. Soft-close doors and drawers.

  13. Cabinets to the ceiling.

  14. Engineered wood flooring to match the rest of the main level.

  15. Approximately 36-inch farmhouse/apron-front sink with unequal double basins: prefer 30/70 or similar, never 50/50. Use a smaller left bowl and larger right bowl in the concept, with a low divider.

  16. Under-sink storage solution for sponges, cleaning supplies, etc.

  17. Island seating: exactly four 18-inch-wide dark-frame stools with brown seats and wood backs, in one hall-side row with no end stools. Lengthen the island to 8 feet and use 24-inch stool centers instead of 20 inches. This gives 6-inch gaps between nominal seats; verify the selected stools and occupied circulation.

  18. Built-in soap dispenser beside the main faucet. The preview shows one pump; retain the original provision for a second hand/dish-soap dispenser when coordinating the final countertop hole layout. Do not pre-drill before selecting the sink and fixtures.

  19. Deep pull-out drawer for plastic containers.

  20. Deep pull-out drawers for plates and bowls.

  21. 36-inch induction cooktop.

  22. Fisher & Paykel 30-inch refrigerator and 30-inch freezer columns separated by the oven/storage tower on the bottom wall: RS3084FLJK1 freezer toward the mudroom wall, RS3084SRHK1 fridge toward the stairs. Handles face the tower. The stainless-panel finish remains a concept. Confirm separate-column installation, handing, panels, ventilation, hinge-side filler, and full door/drawer clearances before ordering.

  23. Microwave and/or air fryer.

  24. Separate hot and cold drinking/filtered-water dispenser beside the main sink faucet, not cold-only. Coordinate the filter, hot-water tank, power, plumbing, and under-sink storage.

  25. Will need a large electrical panel capacity to accommodate appliances running.

  26. Second sink somewhere (like prep kitchen)

  27. Water leak sensor.

Wants

  2. Paper towel holder inside a cabinet

  3. Might have to prep range for gas in case of resale.

  4. Prefer warm wood style kitchen

  5. Drawer with power outlets for phones, tablets, laptops.

  6. Instant drinkable hot water dispenser.

  7. Oversized dishwasher.

  8. Storage on both sides of the island.
