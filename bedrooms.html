Bedrooms

Ground-floor bedroom layout: place the queen bed's headboard against the wall opposite the bedroom entry, facing the door. Shift the bed and both nightstands toward the porch-window end as far as the furniture and trim allow. Keep the windows unobstructed and retain the existing bed size. Final circulation and window access need verification.

Upstairs bedrooms

Front-window revision: coordinate three narrow casement openings in each of Bedrooms 3 and 4 with the new light-masonry front facade, retaining Bedroom 3's west window. Room and closet sizes do not change. Bedroom 4's desk and wall lamp move 1 ft 4.8 in right to x = 22.1 to sit on a solid window pier. Sills/heads remain 3/8 ft above the floor. Verify selected sizes, egress, curtain reach and occupied furniture access; see the upstairs study.

Bedrooms 2, 3 and 4 each use a full-size 54 × 75-inch mattress, one nightstand, a desk and chair, and wall-mounted lamps above the desk and nightstand. The primary uses a queen 60 × 80-inch mattress, two nightstands with wall lamps, a desk, wall TV and two-chair sitting area. See the upstairs furnishing study for scope and verification limits. These sizes do not change the ground-floor guest bed.

Ground-floor guest suite

Keep the nominal 5.5 × 7.4 ft walk-in at x = 19-24.5. The toilet/shower room is separated from the 6.7 × 3.4 ft sink alcove by a full-height partition and 30 × 96-inch pocket door at z = 39.6. The full-height south privacy return at x = 29.26-31.2, z = 43.2 ends flush with the closed vanity's frontmost hardware, screening it without extending past it. Enter beside its west end without a door or spanning header. Facing the sink, the 36-inch vanity has one left cupboard door and three right pull-out drawers. The bedroom retains its nominal 12.2 × 11.2 ft main envelope at z = 43-54.2 plus the 5.5 × 1 ft entry nook; the privacy return occupies a small part of that envelope. Queen bed, both nightstands, bedroom entry, windows, exterior and full hall width stay unchanged; verify occupied bedside circulation.

The walk-in has an approximately 82-inch single-sided west-wall storage run. Use the former north-end gap for 10 additional inches of long hanging; shift the short-hang section and five-shelf folded-clothes bay north without reducing their widths. Long-hang shelf width is 43.6 inches, with a continuous modeled rail approximately 41.2 inches long. The last shelf stops approximately 0.3 inches before the north baseboard shoe; verify field dimensions and fitting tolerance. Reserving a full 24-inch clothing zone still leaves approximately 36.3 inches clear at the opposite baseboard shoe. The doorless entry remains nominally 30 × 96 inches, x = 21.35-23.85. No opposing-wall, hall or bedroom storage is added. Verify finished entry width, actual clothes, supports and occupied retrieval; not accessibility approval. Explore the suite.
