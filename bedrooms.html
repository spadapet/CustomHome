Upstairs bedrooms

Bottom-board ends and face align with the sides, without a projecting stool, horns or separate apron. Recessed opening-width white sills stay 1 inch thick and exactly 6 inches from interior frame face to front at the unchanged room wall. Door casing stays. Frames shift outward only in depth, retaining opening positions/sizes and sill/head datums. Deeper installation structure, insulation, condensation, anchorage, flashing and drainage require professional review; see current window concept.
