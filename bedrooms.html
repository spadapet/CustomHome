Bedrooms

Ground-floor bedroom layout: place the queen bed's headboard against the wall opposite the bedroom entry, facing the door. Shift the bed and both nightstands toward the porch-window end as far as the furniture and trim allow. Keep the windows unobstructed and retain the existing bed size. Final circulation and window access need verification.

Ground-floor guest suite

Widen the guest closet 2 feet west into the hall: x = 17-25, z = 34.6-42, nominally 8 × 7.4 ft. The north hall retains approximately 53.4 inches between finished wall faces; its southern entry area stays full width. Bedroom furniture, bedroom footprint, bathroom and exterior remain fixed. A centered 36 × 84-inch cased opening at x = 19.5-22.5 has no door, avoiding both a swing and an unverified pocket-wall installation.

Full-depth hanging now runs down the left and right walls, with a straight 43.8-inch middle aisle. Each run has approximately 61 inches of continuous rod: long hanging on the left, two short-hanging rods on the right, plus upper shelves. Five 12-inch-deep, 32.4-inch-wide shelves sit on the back wall between the runs. This is possible because the closet was widened; removing a door alone would not have made two full-depth runs fit the former 6-foot width. Confirm framing, actual clothes, supports, hall/bedroom circulation and finished clear widths; not accessibility approval. Explore the suite.

Needs

  • Hang bars for long dresses

  • hang bars for short clothes

  • The hanging bars are a single continuous piece, since bars with extension joints can interfere with smooth sliding.
