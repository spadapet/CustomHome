Bedrooms

Ground-floor bedroom layout: place the queen bed's headboard against the wall opposite the bedroom entry, facing the door. Shift the bed and both nightstands toward the porch-window end as far as the furniture and trim allow. Keep the windows unobstructed and retain the existing bed size. Final circulation and window access need verification.

Ground-floor guest suite

Do not extend the closet into the hall walkway. Restore its original x = 19-25, z = 34.6-42 footprint, nominally 6 × 7.4 ft. The hall retains its full 6.8-foot nominal width, approximately 77.4 inches between finished wall faces. All closet walls, hanging and shelves remain behind x = 19. The doorless 30 × 84-inch cased opening at x = 19.55-22.05 avoids a door swing; bedroom furniture and the exterior stay fixed.

Keep usable hanging without narrowing the hall: a north-wall long-hang run, a shorter double-hang section on the south wall beside the entry, upper shelves, and five 12-inch-deep, 28.8-inch-wide west-wall shelves inside the closet. Two 24-inch-deep north/south clothing zones leave a 36.6-inch middle cross-aisle. Full-depth left/right hanging would leave only about 20 inches in this original footprint, so is not used. Confirm real clothes, supports, finished entry width and occupied access; not accessibility approval. Explore the suite.

Needs

  • Walk in closet as much as possible.

  • Hang bars for long dresses

  • hang bars for short clothes

  • The hanging bars are a single continuous piece, since bars with extension joints can interfere with smooth sliding.

Wants

  1. Shelves

  2. Drawers
