Home vision / Electrical

Electrical

Needs

  1. Whole house CAT6 wiring. Ethernet in: offices, by TVs in living and master.

  2. Ethernet and plugs for TVs should be hidden behind the TV

  3. Multiple Ethernet outlets in office

  4. Car charging plugs in garage

  5. Media closet/hub for all CAT6 wires, fiber, coax (TV antenna), security camera NVR

  6. Wire CAT6 for external and internal POE security cameras.

  7. One high-mounted electrical outlet in the master bedroom, hidden behind the TV

  8. Electrical outlets on the front porch for Christmas lights and other decorations

  9. Outdoor electrical outlets positioned for hanging and seasonal decorations

Wants

  1. Wire for alarm system

  2. TV antenna on roof

  3. At least one electrical outlet in each walk-in closet

  4. Four-outlet receptacles in the bedrooms to reduce the need for extension cords
