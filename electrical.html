Electrical

Kitchen and message-board lighting

Recessed fixture revision: replace the added surface-mounted ceiling disks throughout both modeled floors, including closets, halls and garage, with warm 2700 K-intent, dimmable deep-regressed recessed lights: 4-inch apertures, small white trims, dark 2-inch-deep baffles and frosted lenses recessed 2 inches above the ceiling plane. Broad downward beams serve whole-room ambient lighting only; retain separate piano/reading lamps and existing kitchen layers. This supersedes the surface-mounted fixture description below. Verify ceiling cavity/joist fit, insulation and airtight ratings, garage fire-separation assemblies, wet-location suitability, dimmer compatibility, output/spacing and glare with selected manufacturer data; these are not approved products or construction details.

Whole-home warm-light update: use shielded, frosted 2700 K-intent ceiling fixtures across both modeled floors, closets and halls, plus shaded living end-table and piano task lamps. Keep existing exterior lighting. Recessed cans are for broad whole-room ambient light only, not accent or counter-task spotlights; dedicated kitchen layers handle those tasks. Use independent dimming and CRI 90+ as product-selection targets. Night/Day controls the modeled sources; plans and other floors switch them off. Fixture output, spacing, glare, wet-location ratings, drivers, dimmers and circuit design remain unverified. No basement lighting model is implied.

Provide separate dimmable kitchen layers: four small regressed/baffled recessed fixtures for ambient light, the two island pendants, diffused front-positioned under-cabinet strips at the cooking wall, and the hood task light. Warm 3000 K and CRI 90+ are design targets, not selected products. Do not treat fixture count or the rendered brightness as an approved lighting calculation. Confirm selected IES distributions, lumen output, trim cutoffs, insulation/air-tight ratings as applicable, driver access, compatible dimmers, circuit loads and reflected glare on quartz before installation. See layout and preview behavior.

Add a separately dimmable, shielded picture light above the family message board, nominal 45 inches wide at about 85.2 inches above the floor. Aim down at the writing/cork surfaces rather than at occupants; test reflection on the existing glass before fixing. Verify wall anchorage, feed/driver access, projection/headroom and dimming range. The viewer's Night button energizes the kitchen/board layers and enables island visibility; it is not a selected physical switch or automation system.

Built-in bathroom night lighting

Interior stair LED strips: continuous warm 2700 K diffused strips beneath the main upstairs stair tread edges, inset 1.5 inches at each side, now glow and illuminate the steps in Night mode by default. These are strips, not exposed individual LED dots. Day and plan/exterior/structural-preview views disable them. Garage steps remain without LEDs; basement flights are unchanged. Conceal sources from normal sight lines and verify channels, wiring, accessible drivers, dimming, glare and required stair illumination with the electrician; rendered brightness is not a safety-lighting calculation.

Provide a dim, warm built-in night light in every bathroom and powder room on every floor, independent of the main lights. A switch-integrated night light is a candidate, not a selected product; illuminated receptacles or low-level vanity/toe-kick lights are alternatives. Prefer dusk/motion operation. Confirm location, brightness, circuit/control compatibility, GFCI protection and wet-location restrictions with the electrician. See bathroom requirements.
