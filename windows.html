Home vision / Windows

Windows

Good quality windows that are easy to maintain, clean. Stay standard size as much as possible. Something easy to get window treatments.

Selected direction: Andersen 100 Series casement windows throughout, Black exterior and White interior, with white interior trim. Prefer standard catalog sizes, not custom units. The 3D preview uses simplified closed casements with separate sash frames, clear panes, and white crank/lock hardware; it is not manufacturer CAD. Wide openings use grouped casements, and the tall bay beside the dining patio door uses stacked casements. Existing wall openings are retained as provisional design envelopes, not verified standard unit or rough-opening dimensions. Final unit sizes, combinations, handing, egress, safety glazing, and hardware require dealer/architect confirmation. Entry-door sidelights remain fixed door glazing with matching black/white finishes, not standard casement units; door leaves are unchanged.

Manufacturer references: 100 Series casement product and sizing resources, Andersen Revit/BIM guidance and standard-size type catalogs, and 100 Series casement BIM model listing. No manufacturer model files have been imported into this preview.

Needs

Interior trim style: painted white, craftsman-inspired casing like the supplied reference. Use wide flat side boards, a deeper header with a small projecting cap and bead, and a projecting window sill with an apron below. The 3D concept uses provisional 3 1/2-inch side boards and 5 1/2-inch headers; final profiles, cabinet junctions, and dimensions need finish-carpentry coordination. Coordinate with the selected Black exterior/White interior window finishes; door leaves are unchanged.

Kitchen sink wall: retain the centered provisional 66 × 42-inch opening and two provisional 18 × 42-inch side openings, now with casement concepts. Leave 12-inch wall piers between openings and match the white craftsman casing. All three openings have a 51-inch bottom and 7 ft 9 in head. Retain the 8-inch-deep quartz plant sills, central faucet notch, and matching quartz backsplash beneath. The wood aprons clear the modeled counter by approximately 10 inches. Verify sealed joints, moisture-resistant trim, hardware reach, structural framing, and standard Andersen 100 units and rough openings before construction.

  • Many windows for a bright house with natural sunlight

  • Avoid custom size windows

  • Windows with windows sill

  • Windows with trim around them, inside and outside

  • Wired for automated shades.

  • Frosted windows in bathroom or walk in closet if they have windows.

  • Kids rooms do not have low windows
