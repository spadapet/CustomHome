Upstairs

Upstairs 3D study

All upstairs bedroom, bathroom, closet and service-room doorways have nominal 8-foot (96-inch) heads, measured above the upstairs floor. Casing follows the taller openings. Door leaf products and swings remain undetailed where only an opening is shown; see the house-wide door requirement.

The hall stairs ascend from the living-room side and descend to the basement from the powder-room side. The upstairs arrival is on the powder-room side of the opening; its landing, hall connection, and guard opening follow that flight. Stair dimensions and the overall opening are unchanged.
