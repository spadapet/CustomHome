Garage

Needs

  1. Large two car garage

  2. Space for truck GMC Sierra and sedan. Maximize allowed door sizes for Birmingham, which is two 9’ doors.

  3. Space in front and side of the vehicles for tools, bikes, lawn mower

  4. High voltage plugs for car chargers

  5. Drive straight into the garage, no driveway twists

Wants

  1. One EV charger on the interior wall pier between the two garage doors, not beside the house door or on a side wall. Keep the cable coiled off the floor. Verify electrical supply, mounting, protection, and cable reach to both vehicles.

  2. Five bikes in roll-in floor-level wheel racks, with both wheels on the floor. No hanging hooks or vertical lifting. Keep the alcove aisle clear; remove the fixed workbench that blocked access.

  3. Painted walls and ceiling

  4. Epoxy coated garage floor

Working layout

Storage above the bikes

View the storage above the bikes. Add a raised 8 × 4 ft goods-only storage-lift concept above the existing five floor-parked bikes, centered at (50.4, 18.5) ft. It provides 32 sq ft of storage deck at 7 ft 6 in above the garage slab, with a 3-inch platform allowance and 7 ft 3 in modeled underside clearance. Eight illustrative seasonal bins sit above it; their tops remain below the nominal ceiling allowance. Visibility → Bike storage lift can hide the assembly without moving the bikes.

There are only about 33 inches between the raised deck and nominal ceiling: this cannot be a walk-on loft, even with bunk-style stairs. Keep it goods-only. No roof changes, mezzanine or upstairs cuts are proposed.

Storage access view shows a guarded mobile platform-stair concept after the truck and all bikes have been moved outside. It has a 30-inch-square standing platform approximately 3 ft 6 in above the floor, six nominal 7-inch rises, five 10-inch treads, continuous handrails, a 42-inch platform guard and locking-caster allowances. The footprint is approximately 2 ft 6 in × 6 ft 8 in, with about 81 inches nominal headroom above the standing platform. Reach bins from this platform; never climb onto the goods deck. Normal parking view hides the access unit and restores the truck/bikes. This is not an always-deployed stair; its safe stowed location is not selected.

Choose either a manufacturer-approved lowering/loading procedure with the whole travel zone cleared, or a manufacturer-approved static retrieval procedure with the raised deck positively secured and all movement disabled. Never use the steps beside a moving lift. Actual mobile-step product, rated load/reach, brakes, guards, finished headroom, clearance from overhead services, secure lift supports, safe stowage, handling heavy bins and structural/fire review remain unresolved. No people-carrying lift, structural capacity or construction approval is implied.

Parking and access

Garage doors and trim: keep both nominal 9 × 8 ft openings. Use a warm off-white cream door finish, modeled as #E6D7B9, with subtle cream panel detailing and retained dark handles/glazing. Add visible warm-taupe surrounds (#A18D70): 6-inch side pieces, an 8-inch header, and a projecting cap. The main trim is 3 inches deep, mounted outside the opening so its nominal width/height do not shrink. These are screen colors and provisional profiles, not selected products; confirm samples, anchorage, flashing, seals, and door travel.

  • Keep the full-size Sierra EV in the right bay, now aligned with its 9-foot door at (48.5, 38), 6 inches farther right. Keep the BMW left, but move it 6 inches farther into the garage to (37, 40). BMW rear-to-inside-wall allowance improves from about 5.7 to 11.7 inches, while retaining approximately 36.5 inches ahead to equipment projections in its lane. Truck nose/rear allowances remain approximately 14/13 inches; truck-side mirror clearance is about 34 inches. These are modest parking improvements, not a larger garage, vehicle-door clearance or turning simulation.
  • The center mirror aisle increases from approximately 43.4 to 49.4 inches; truck-body separation from the mudroom steps increases by 6 inches to approximately 30 inches where their footprints approach. Keep the mudroom/exterior walls, vehicle sizes, door openings, bikes and charger unchanged. Real overhead-door tracks, steering paths, passenger-door sweeps, parking sensors and stops need checking; rear bumper allowances are not walking routes.
  • Keep the equipment station on the house-side wall in front of the BMW, not beside the truck. The conventional mower now rests on four wheels, with its handle fully unfolded, centered approximately (30.65, 28.615); its grip extends to about z = 31.865. No upright-storage capability or fuel type is assumed. Move the rolling vacuum to the right end of the station near (35.05, 28.115). The leaf blower has an obvious dedicated wall cradle at the left side of the tool board, with its body 5 ft 4 in above the garage slab and nozzle clear of the lower shelf. Retain the tools, charging shelf and upper bins. Verify the owner's actual equipment envelopes, fuel/charging instructions, retrieval, vehicle doors and a 36-inch cross-aisle.
  • Retain the house-entry opening and steps at x = 39.5-42.5 ft, nominal 36 × 96 inches. Both vehicle doors remain 8 feet tall. Use the revised parked positions above without moving openings or shrinking either vehicle.
  • Retain two straight steps aligned with the shifted doorway: provisional 15-inch level change, two 7.5-inch rises, two 36-inch-wide × 11-inch-deep tread surfaces, upper at house level, with contrasting nosings. No separate landing/rails are modeled; this is not installation approval or a step-free route. The mudroom bench and kitchen opening stay fixed. Confirm surveyed levels, drainage, rated/self-closing/latching door, safe swing, required landing/handrails and garage fire separation with the architect.
