Garage

Needs

  1. Large two car garage

  2. Space for truck GMC Sierra and sedan. Maximize allowed door sizes for Birmingham, which is two 9’ doors.

  3. Space in front and side of the vehicles for tools, bikes, lawn mower

  4. High voltage plugs for car chargers

  5. Drive straight into the garage, no driveway twists

Wants

  1. One EV charger on the interior wall pier between the two garage doors, not beside the house door or on a side wall. Keep the cable coiled off the floor. Verify electrical supply, mounting, protection, and cable reach to both vehicles.

  2. Five bikes in roll-in floor-level wheel racks, with both wheels on the floor. No hanging hooks or vertical lifting. Keep the alcove aisle clear; remove the fixed workbench that blocked access.

  3. Painted walls and ceiling

  4. Epoxy coated garage floor

Working layout

Storage above the bikes

View the storage above the bikes. Add a raised 8 × 4 ft goods-only storage-lift concept above the existing five floor-parked bikes, centered at (50.4, 18.5) ft. It provides 32 sq ft of storage deck at 7 ft 6 in above the garage slab, with a 3-inch platform allowance and 7 ft 3 in modeled underside clearance. Eight illustrative seasonal bins sit above it; their tops remain below the nominal ceiling allowance. Visibility → Bike storage lift can hide the assembly without moving the bikes.

This is the space-efficient alternative to a walk-up loft, not a mezzanine or a people-carrying lift. The alcove is about 10 × 12 ft and the model has roughly 10 ft 3 in from slab to nominal ceiling level. A walk-up platform would require a separate study of taller ceiling/roof volume, upstairs conflicts, stair footprint, landing/headroom, guards and bike roll-out. No stairs, roof changes or upstairs-floor cuts are included.

Only the raised storage position is shown; the suspension and drive housing are schematic, not a designed mechanism. A real system must lower for loading only after all bikes and people are cleared from its full travel area. Do not stand on or under a moving platform. Product selection, rated capacity, load restraint, travel/obstruction controls, positive holding safeguards, ceiling structure/anchorage, fire separation, lighting/services and manufacturer installation/operation requirements remain unverified. No capacity or construction approval is implied.

Parking and access

Garage doors and trim: keep both nominal 9 × 8 ft openings. Use a warm off-white cream door finish, modeled as #E6D7B9, with subtle cream panel detailing and retained dark handles/glazing. Add visible warm-taupe surrounds (#A18D70): 6-inch side pieces, an 8-inch header, and a projecting cap. The main trim is 3 inches deep, mounted outside the opening so its nominal width/height do not shrink. These are screen colors and provisional profiles, not selected products; confirm samples, anchorage, flashing, seals, and door travel.

  • Swap the vehicles: Sierra EV in the right bay at (48, 38), BMW in the left bay at (37, 40.5). Keep their dimensions and front/back parking positions. Truck nose/rear gaps remain approximately 14/13 inches; BMW rear clearance is approximately 6 inches and requires garage-door hardware verification. Removing the landing frees the front cross-aisle beside the BMW. Rear gaps are not walking routes.
  • Use the approximately 43.4-inch center aisle between mirrors and the clear floor in front of the BMW to reach the two straight house-entry steps. Five bikes stay against the alcove's back wall with their roll-out floor unchanged. Confirm actual vehicle-door, handlebar, rack, and maneuvering envelopes.
  • Move the complete equipment station from beside the truck to the house-side wall in front of the BMW: upright-storage battery mower, rolling shop vac, blower, garden tools, hand-tool board, battery shelf, and upper bins. The conservative front gap is approximately 42.5 inches, replacing the former 15.7-inch truck-side pinch point; keep a 36-inch access route clear. Removing the station restores approximately 40 inches beside the truck's mirror envelope. Preserve vehicle parking, bikes, charger, steps, and pedestrian-door access. Confirm actual equipment retrieval/turning, vehicle-door swings, anchorage, and battery charging guidance; these are closed-object concept dimensions.
  • Move the house-entry doorway and both steps one foot away from the Sierra, toward the BMW side. Keep the 36-inch opening aligned with the steps at x = 40–43 ft; preserve vehicle parking and step dimensions.
  • Show two straight steps up to the house door, with no separate landing, rails, or guards. Use a provisional 15-inch garage-to-house difference: two 7.5-inch rises and two 36-inch-wide, 11-inch-deep tread surfaces, the upper at house level. The steps project approximately 22 inches into the garage, aligned with the doorway, rather than turning into a parking bay. Keep contrasting nosings and the mudroom bench's existing position. Floors, garage furnishings, exterior openings, approach pads, and tour elevations follow the revised slab level. Surveyed levels, drainage, door swing, required landings/handrails, and garage/house fire separation need architect/code review; omitting them in this concept is not installation approval or a step-free route.
