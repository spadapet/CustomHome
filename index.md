# Spada Custom Home

### Vision

We envision a warm, inviting farmhouse-style home of approximately 3,500 to 4,000 square feet, thoughtfully designed around the everyday needs of our family of five. With three elementary-aged children and two working parents, we want a home that feels beautiful and comfortable while also being highly functional for family life, work, and learning.

The heart of the home should be a spacious, welcoming kitchen with plenty of room for cooking together, gathering, and everyday family meals. We want generous storage, a large island, and an easy connection between the kitchen, pantry, mudroom, garage, dining, and main living spaces.

Because both parents work from home, we need two dedicated offices. One should function as a private work-from-home office, while the second should be flexible enough to serve as another professional workspace as well as a shared homework and study room for the children. The goal is to create spaces where parents can work and children can learn without disrupting the main family areas.

The garage should comfortably accommodate two vehicles without feeling tight, while providing substantial additional space for bikes, scooters, sports equipment, tools, and everyday family storage. We want the transition from the garage into the home to be practical, with convenient access to a mudroom, pantry, and kitchen.

Throughout the house, we want comfortable, appropriately sized spaces with good storage, natural light, and an easy flow between rooms.

Above all, we want the home to feel warm, timeless, and welcoming. The farmhouse character should be refined and natural rather than overly rustic, creating a home that works beautifully for our family today and continues to work as our children grow.
