Move first-floor rooms and walls

Start the local preview with node preview-server.cjs, open the first-floor designer, or select First-floor designer in the 3D studio. Drag rooms or walls, resize rooms with the corner handle, or use the dimension fields in feet. Connected wall endpoints and shared room edges follow edits. Window and door openings keep their widths; the stairs stay fixed.

Use snap, zoom, Fit plan, Undo and Redo as you work. Arrow keys move a selected wall; Shift moves four snap steps. Completed changes save automatically to ground-layout.json, separately from upstairs. Wait for the saved status before closing. Save errors leave your edit in the tab and explain the problem; another tab's newer layout cannot be silently overwritten.

View changes in 3D shows edited floors, walls and openings as a structure-only study. Furniture, cabinetry, fixtures and tours are hidden, not coordinated. Upstairs and exterior remain separate concepts. Reset original layout restores the furnished ground floor and can be undone. Static hosting can preview edits but cannot save them to the project. The source-plan areas, window counts and room studies below are baseline figures, not recalculated schedules for your edits.

House square footage

The source-plan figures give approximately 3,637 sq ft; the adapted default 3D model envelopes give approximately 3,974 sq ft. These are different, unreconciled measurement bases, not interchangeable house-size figures. Both exclude garage and outdoor spaces; neither includes the basement. The model's 1,926.91 sq ft downstairs includes partitions, circulation, and stair footprints, but not the front porch or covered patio. Its known 29 sq ft kitchen/prep extension explains only part of the 98.01 sq ft downstairs difference; upstairs differs by 238.63 sq ft. Browser-saved upstairs edits are not included. Neither basis is certified living area or a zoning calculation. See the measurement rules and reproducible calculations for the six-rectangle first-floor breakdown and exclusions.
