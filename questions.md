# Questions

- [ ] How much of the driveway should be reused?

- [ ] Does the driveway entrance need to be shifted over?

- [ ] What trees can be removed?

- [ ] How is the builder paid, fixed fee above actual cost?

- [ ] How is the house value computed for paying property taxes?

- [ ] What to do about major HVAC items: furnace, a/c, water heater? Sell or reuse?

- [ ] For engineering and design, if things change, do we get to keep the floor plan?

- [ ] Can the electrical wires going to the house be buried?

- [ ] For cost-plus model, is the percentage added to labor, or labor and materials?

- [ ] For appliances and other selections (lights, hardware) are there allowances given? What happens if we are under or over the allowance?
