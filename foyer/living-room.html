Living Room

We’re aiming for a bright, modern focal wall with a wide horizontal gas fireplace flanked by two windows, with a stone look around the fireplace.

Needs

  1. Try a U-shaped double-chaise sectional arranged chaise–seat–seat–chaise: exactly two regular seats between the long end chaises, facing the fireplace. Remove separate chairs and ottomans from this study. Use warm tan/brown upholstery. This supersedes the earlier no-sectional direction; four primary positions do not establish the previous six-person comfort target. Keep the earlier sofa/chair layouts available for comparison.

  2. Wider gas fireplace.

  3. Five living-room window openings: retain the two 48 × 64.8-inch fireplace-side openings at 31.2-inch sills. Group three matching 30 × 60-inch return-wall windows at 40-inch centers, with 36-inch sills: casement / fixed picture / casement. All heads align at 8 ft under the 9 ft ceiling. Keep sconces at the outer ends of the group, not between windows; the pair on the removed stair partition is removed. See the window schedule, positions, spacing, and trim dimensions; sizes and framing remain provisional.

  4. Stone covering the fireplace surround from floor to ceiling, including the side returns. Match the supplied fireplace photo's deeper taupe-brown stacked stone, with muted earthy brown, weathered gray, and charcoal variation and dark warm-gray joints, replacing the earlier golden honey-beige direction. Keep the staggered rectangular stones, subtle texture, exposed firebox, and TV. Photo lighting affects the apparent color; this is a screen approximation, not an identified stone product. Confirm physical samples, installation, and appliance clearances. See the mantel and window proportions below.

  5. The former long fireplace-side cabinet runs stay removed; use the narrower matching media cabinets specified below instead, with no replacement benches. Keep all four open under-stair bookcase bays, shelves, and books through the end, with no solid end infill or stair-end door; continue exposed baseboard and shoe to the west wall. Keep the stairwell window removed.

  6. Add a simple white-oak beam mantel above the firebox, approximately 74.4 inches wide and 3.6 inches thick, with its underside at 48 inches above the floor. Show visible amber flames with bright cores over an ember bed. The mantel height/projection, nearby wood cabinetry, TV heat exposure, firebox depth, venting, and all combustible clearances are provisional, not an approved fireplace installation.

  7. Extra blocking behind TV over fireplace for hanging bracket.

  8. Matching closed-door white-oak cabinets directly adjoining both sides of the fireplace stone with no lateral gaps. Each is approximately 24 × 11.76 × 26 inches wide/deep/high, narrowed from 36 inches, below the window aprons. Door fronts and top leading edges line up with the stone face, not the projecting mantel. Two narrower Shaker doors per cabinet use concealed push latches; consoles, controllers, chargers, phones, iPads and laptops stay inside, subject to equipment-fit verification in the smaller storage. Keep the tops clear, with no exposed bays or drawers.

  9. Concealed receptacles, replaceable USB-C PD charging and Ethernet inside the cabinets, with at least 3 HDMI runs (or accessible conduit sized for future HDMI) to the TV/fireplace location (Xbox, Switch and security cameras). Retain 3-inch rear service gaps and open service backs. Verify actual equipment/laptop fit, cable bends and concealed ventilation for closed-door operation; the open rear alone does not establish adequate airflow. Direct stone adjacency is a visual concept, not approved combustible clearance; verify selected-fireplace requirements and noncombustible junctions before fabrication, along with charging loads, anchorage, window access and chair/door clearance.

  10. Engineered hardwood throughout. At least 7” plank width.

Wants

  1. Similar overall look/feel to the reference photo.

  2. Floor outlet near primary seating area.

Connected kids room

Swap the kids room with the stairs. The playroom occupies the former central stair area, x = 19-29 ft, now extended to z = 24.7367-34.6 ft, approximately 10 × 9.86 ft. Its kitchen-facing wall projects 11.56 inches farther forward to align with the tall cabinet fronts; the doorway stays fixed. The stacked stairs move to the west-side former playroom and turn toward the hall. Remove the living-facing partition and replace it with a 42-inch-high oak-capped dark guard at the stairwell edge, with vertical balusters and clear gaps no greater than 3.75 inches. Keep the east hall approach open, existing stair rails and under-stair bookcases intact. Verify wall-removal support, guard anchorage and local safety requirements before construction. The kids' doorless 4 ft 6 in-wide, 8 ft-high entry faces west at x = 19 ft, z = 27-31.5 ft, with white Craftsman trim. The former exterior kids window now lights the stairwell; this interior playroom has no modeled exterior window. No direct sofa supervision or acoustic separation is claimed.
