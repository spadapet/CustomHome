The lot

75 ft wide × 130 ft deep (9,750 sq ft).

Current 3D house placement

Open the lot plan. The house is shifted approximately 3.86 ft east from the former centered placement, with the porch and garage facing the front/street. Leave 5 ft clear from the east exterior stone wall to the inside edge of the side fence rails. The rear exterior stone-wall face remains 30 ft from the back property line; the lot stays 75 × 130 ft.

  • Side-wall clearances to property lines: approximately 5.67 ft east and 13.38 ft west. The east wall-to-fence clearance is 5 ft, not a 5 ft property-line setback; fence centerline is 5.17 ft from the wall. Roof projections and actual zoning setbacks require separate review.
  • Front boundary to porch slab edge: approximately 36.48 ft. Steps and roof projections extend farther forward.
  • Rear boundary to the original house roof overhang: approximately 29.58 ft. Patio/step rear limits stay approximately 30.23 ft from the boundary. The separate 3.5 ft-wide garden walkway now follows the rear fence, at z = -31.075 to -27.575 ft, leaving lawn toward the house except for the patio connector. Its outer edge is 1.25 ft inside the rear lot boundary, including the fence inset/drainage strip. House placement remains a wall-face measurement, not site or zoning approval.
  • The driveway extends to the front lot boundary. Remove the separate straight entry walk to that boundary; use the garage/driveway and porch-side paver connection for front-door access. A public sidewalk, curb, and street right-of-way are not modeled.

The site retains the 29 × 9.1 ft outdoor patio limit, rear/side fence and gates, and a 3 ft 6 in fence-edge walkway with connectors to the patio and gates. The front yard stays open. Camera/light placements, site rules, and covered-patio/fence setbacks remain to verify.

This is a placement study, not a survey or zoning approval. Confirm actual boundaries, easements, required setbacks and projection allowances, lot coverage, height, drainage, grade, and driveway access with the architect and city before fixing the house location.
