An **opening** is one window group in a wall. A **unit** is one casement or fixed picture section within that group. A paired casement counts as one opening and two units. The garden-facing dining window has two side-by-side casements; patio-door glazing is excluded.

## Scope and sources

- Excludes glazing in entry, patio and interior doors, and the eight garage-door panes.
- Basement windows and skylights are not modeled and are not included.
- The separate exterior visualization has 29 concept window openings representing the same house, **not additional windows**. Count each joined kitchen/prep bank as one opening containing three units. Other placeholder divisions are not fully reconciled with the furnished models, particularly the combined dining door/window assembly.
- Main kitchen retains its two 18 x 42 in casements and 54 x 42 in picture unit, joined edge-to-edge with one slim perimeter surround. One 90 in assembly replaces three separated openings; the former two 9 in wall piers are removed conceptually. Factory joining, header/structure, actual rough opening and operation remain unapproved. See the [kitchen schedule](../windows.html#kitchen-sink-window-schedule).
- Prep has one joined standard 2050 / 5050 / 2050 counter bank with a single perimeter trim/sill and a separate standard 2060 aisle-end casement. Frame heights are 59.5 in over the counter and 71.5 in at the end wall. The joined assembly and wider structural opening remain unapproved. See the [prep window schedule](../windows.html#prep-kitchen-window-schedule).
- Garage windows are three 72 x 18 in fixed pictures, sill/head 90/108 in above the garage slab (75/93 in above house datum). They preserve the lower wall for storage; glazing, structure, property-line limits and daylight performance remain unapproved.
- The modeled powder window is now standard size 2640: approximately 2.4583 x 3.9583 ft frame in a minimum 2.5 x 4 ft opening, bottom at 4 ft and top at 8 ft, with lower-half frosting to 6 ft. This replaces the shorter 2630 without changing the count; see the [options and placement in feet](../floor-plan-reference.md#powder-room-window-options-in-feet). Half-frosted product availability, installation and site/nighttime privacy are unverified.
- This is a model takeoff, not a supplier order or egress/ventilation approval. Final unit sizes, mulled assemblies, rough openings, safety glazing and egress remain unverified.
