# House window count

Verified October 24, 2026 against the furnished ground-floor and upstairs 3D models, including hidden room groups.

**31 window openings containing 48 individual modeled units: 42 operable casements and 6 fixed picture windows.**

Includes three high garage pictures, one prep picture window replacing two sink-wall casements and one standard-size single-casement powder window replacing the pair; the former two-unit ground stairwell window was previously removed.

An **opening** is one window group in a wall. A **unit** is one casement or fixed picture section within that group. A paired casement counts as one opening and two units. The window beside the dining patio door counts as one opening with two stacked casements; the door itself is excluded.

## Scope and sources

- Excludes glazing in entry, patio and interior doors, and the eight garage-door panes.
- Basement windows and skylights are not modeled and are not included.
- The separate exterior visualization has 31 concept window groups representing the same house, **not additional windows**. Other placeholder divisions are not fully reconciled with the furnished models, particularly the combined dining door/window assembly.
- Garage windows are three 72 x 18 in fixed pictures, sill/head 90/108 in above the garage slab (75/93 in above house datum). They preserve the lower wall for storage; glazing, structure, property-line limits and daylight performance remain unapproved.
- The modeled powder window is now standard size 2640: approximately 2.4583 x 3.9583 ft frame in a minimum 2.5 x 4 ft opening, bottom at 4 ft and top at 8 ft, with lower-half frosting to 6 ft. This replaces the shorter 2630 without changing the count; see the [options and placement in feet](../floor-plan-reference.md#powder-room-window-options-in-feet). Half-frosted product availability, installation and site/nighttime privacy are unverified.
- This is a model takeoff, not a supplier order or egress/ventilation approval. Final unit sizes, mulled assemblies, rough openings, safety glazing and egress remain unverified.
