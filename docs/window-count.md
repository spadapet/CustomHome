# House window count

Verified October 18, 2026 against the furnished ground-floor and upstairs 3D models, including hidden room groups.

**29 window openings containing 47 individual modeled units: 45 operable casements and 2 fixed picture windows.**

An **opening** is one window group in a wall. A **unit** is one casement or fixed picture section within that group. A paired casement counts as one opening and two units. The window beside the dining patio door counts as one opening with two stacked casements; the door itself is excluded.

## Scope and sources

- Excludes glazing in entry, patio and interior doors, and the eight garage-door panes.
- Basement windows and skylights are not modeled and are not included.
- The separate exterior visualization has 29 concept window groups representing the same house, **not additional windows**. Its placeholder divisions are not fully reconciled with the furnished models, particularly the combined dining door/window assembly.
- This is a model takeoff, not a supplier order or egress/ventilation approval. Final unit sizes, mulled assemblies, rough openings, safety glazing and egress remain unverified.
