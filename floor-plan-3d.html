    01

    Living room

    An extra-deep, straight four-seat sofa (no sectional or chaise) and two cushioned lounge chairs give the family six seats, all in warm tan/light brown rather than white. The sofa is modeled at 122 inches wide by 54 inches deep, pending product selection. For life with kids, target wipe-clean protected leather or stain-resistant performance fabric; confirm care instructions and samples before selecting a product. No area rug: the honey-oak floor stays exposed beneath the seating. No coffee table or plants. Side windows remain part of the layout.

    02

    Dining room

    A walnut Seno table and six Nosh walnut-and-black-leather chairs add contrast against the honey-oak floor without introducing white upholstery. Two chairs sit on each long side and one at each end. All six move just 2 inches outward from the previous fully tucked positions; the side pairs also spread 1.25 inches each to fit the wider chairs. This remains a mostly tucked layout, not occupied seating clearance. A tall fixed picture window sits beside a single outward-opening glass patio door, shown partly open toward the patio. The rear window and closed exterior corner wall remain.

    03

    A built-in island

    Inspired by your references: a warm-white paneled base, square corner posts, and painted apron support satin-finish warm ivory quartz with warm-gray veining. Matching quartz continues across the kitchen and prep counters. Five backless stools have round light brown cushions, tapered warm-wood legs, circular brass footrests, and brass leg caps. They are pushed in to the island base, three along the long side and one at each end, clear of the corner posts. Pendants remain approximately 54 inches above the countertop. Matching 18-inch upper cabinets flank the sink window with equal gaps and aligned tops.

    04

    Prep + family entry

    A pantry wall, coffee station, and countertop oven keep prep work separate. Matching hardwood continues from the main kitchen into the prep kitchen. The tiled mudroom has six open shoe cubbies, twelve hooks, coat/vacuum storage, a drop zone, and a future stacked-laundry reservation.

    05

    The quieter rooms

    A play-and-reading room, oak office desk with a reading corner, queen guest bed, walk-in storage, two toilet/vanity layouts, linen storage, entry console, and porch benches complete this pass.

    06

    Your two EVs

    Sierra EV on the right, BMW i4 on the left. Two open 9-foot garage bays, two wall chargers, six vertically stored bikes, and a tool bench keep the parking area organized. Vehicle shapes are approximate, sized using the references below.
