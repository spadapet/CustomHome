    Living room

    Dining room

    A walnut Seno table and six Nosh walnut-and-black-leather chairs add contrast against the honey-oak floor without introducing white upholstery. The rear window and closed exterior corner wall remain.

    03

    A simple waterfall island

    A plain white-oak cabinet base and quartz waterfall ends replace the decorative paneled island. Exactly four dark-frame stools with brown seats and wood backs form one row on the hall side; no end stools. The 7-foot length gives compact 20-inch centers, not verified comfortable four-adult spacing. Its 2-foot living-room shift and pendants remain. Use Visibility to hide the island lights independently of the dining light. The Left freezer button operates the left stainless appliance without changing your view.

    04

    Prep + family entry

    A pantry wall, coffee station, and countertop oven keep prep work separate. Matching hardwood continues from the main kitchen into the prep kitchen. The tiled mudroom has six open shoe cubbies, twelve hooks, coat/vacuum storage, a drop zone, and a future stacked-laundry reservation.

    05

    The quieter rooms

    A play-and-reading room, oak office desk with a reading corner, queen guest bed, walk-in storage, two toilet/vanity layouts, linen storage, entry console, and porch benches complete this pass.

    06

    Your two EVs

    Sierra EV on the right, BMW i4 on the left. Two open 9-foot garage bays, two wall chargers, six vertically stored bikes, and a tool bench keep the parking area organized. Vehicle shapes are approximate, sized using the references below.
